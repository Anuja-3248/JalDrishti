Satellite Detection

Detect potential oil spills from SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) satellite imagery. Unlike optical cameras, SAR penetrates clouds and works day & night — making it ideal for continuous ocean surveillance.

Step 02

Slick Analysis

Analyze the oil slick's precise geographic location, shape, area, and surface characteristics. Differentiate between mineral oil and biogenic look-alikes using spectral and texture features.
